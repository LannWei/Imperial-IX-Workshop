01About

About the series

Two connected themes: AI for Science and Lab Automation

The Collaborative Lab Automation Workshop Series is organised by the I-X Centre for AI in Science at Imperial College London. It brings together researchers and practitioners from AI, robotics and the experimental sciences to explore how agentic and embodied AI can support the scientific research cycle: from hypothesis generation and experimental design to robotic execution, data analysis and iterative discovery.

Discussions will focus on how advances in large language models, autonomous agents, robotic manipulation and multi-agent systems can enable intelligent, adaptable laboratory automation, with applications across bioengineering, drug discovery, materials science and chemical engineering.

We are bringing together a mix of senior researchers, early-career researchers, postdocs and PhD students, with perspectives from both academia and industry.

Theme 01

AI for Science

Large language models, autonomous agents and multi-agent systems that reason over literature and data, generate hypotheses and plan experiments.

Theme 02

Lab Automation

Embodied AI and robotic manipulation for intelligent, adaptable laboratories that run experiments, measure outcomes and learn from them.

Topics

  • Large language models
  • Autonomous agents
  • Robotic manipulation
  • Multi-agent systems

Applications

  • Bioengineering
  • Drug discovery
  • Materials science
  • Chemical engineering

03For speakers

Information for speakers

Thank you for considering our invitation

We would be delighted if you could share your research and perspectives with our interdisciplinary community. Here is what speaking in the series involves.

Your talk

Around 30 minutes, then discussion

A talk on your research or professional work and its relevance to AI for Science and Lab Automation, followed by questions and discussion.

Participation

In person or online

All events are hybrid. You are welcome to speak in person at Imperial College London or to join us online, whichever suits you best.

Scheduling

A date that works for you

Once you let us know you are interested, we will follow up by email to agree a suitable date, the talk details and your preferred mode of participation.

Audience

An interdisciplinary community

Researchers and practitioners from AI, robotics and the experimental sciences, with seminar invitations circulated across Imperial College London.

Interested in speaking?

Simply reply to your invitation email, or write to the organisers. We would be delighted to hear from you.
